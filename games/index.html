Games

Two standalone games pulled out of the RetroVerse Arcade. Each is a single index.html — no build step, no framework, no dependencies, no server. Everything renders to one canvas, and progress is kept in localStorage on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Cyber Sweeper

KRONOS-9 Protocol

Minesweeper, overengineered. A five-floor campaign where the mines fight back — subterranean nanite robots burrow between tiles, rage timers count down on the board, and a deployable EMP shockwave buys you a few seconds of quiet when the floor turns against you.

Cyber Dino

Jetpack Joyride

An endless runner that starts on foot in the badlands and does not stay there. Team up with a sleeping pterodactyl to get airborne, pick up crates that morph you into a hydraulic mech, and hold off the KRONOS flying mech suit at the end of the third sector.
