GastRotator is an AI-powered recipe manager for the kinetic kitchen. It uses Google Gemini AI to analyze YouTube transcripts and extract structured recipes, nutritional data, and cooking steps instantly.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

Built as a local-first, privacy-centric application, GastRotator ensures all your recipes and data stay on your device. It features a high-impact, editorial-style interface designed for focus during active cooking, with automated conversions for nutritional intelligence.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Kinetic Feast UI (Flutter)
  ↓ providers
Import Manager (YoutubeExplode / Gemini)
  ↓ persists
Local Storage (SQLite / Encrypted Prefs)

KEY CAPABILITIES

• Magic YouTube Import: Simply share a video to extract a full, structured recipe via AI.
• Nutritional Intelligence: Automated estimation of calories and weight in SI units.
• Editorial Design: Monochromatic depth and asymmetric layouts for a premium digital cookbook feel.
