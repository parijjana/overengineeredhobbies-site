Kalkra is a high-performance mental math trainer built for speed, accuracy, and security. It features an adaptive math engine that calibrates in real-time to the user's performance, offering both solo training and competitive multiplayer matches.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

Kalkra prioritizes integrity and security, utilizing hardware-backed keys and HMAC validation for all local save files. It's designed for serious practitioners of mental arithmetic who value detailed performance tracking and achievement systems.
