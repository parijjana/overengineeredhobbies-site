KALKRA — MENTAL MATH GAME

NEED A HAND? Kalkra is a small, independent project. Email us and a real human will get back to you as soon as we can.

1. CONTACT

For questions, help, bug reports, or feedback, email overengineeredhobbies@gmail.com. Please include your device and its macOS, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened — it helps us respond faster.

2. COMMON QUESTIONS

Is Kalkra online or multiplayer?
No. Kalkra is fully offline and single-player. It needs no account and no internet connection to play.

Does Kalkra collect my data?
No. Kalkra collects no personal data, shows no ads, and includes no tracking or analytics. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where is my progress saved?
Your progress, stats, and achievements are stored locally on your device only — they never leave it. Removing the app clears this local data.

Which platforms is Kalkra on?
Kalkra is on the App Store for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It is free, and one download covers all three. More platforms are planned.

3. BUGS & FEEDBACK

Found a bug or have an idea? Email us at overengineeredhobbies@gmail.com, or open an issue on the project's GitHub repository: github.com/parijjana/Kalkra.

4. PRIVACY

Kalkra is built to respect your privacy. Read the full Privacy Policy.
