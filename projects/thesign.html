The Sign is a minimalist 2D puzzle-platformer rendered in the style of public-safety signage. It features a language-free experience where everything from puzzles to navigation is communicated through symbols, teaching real-world concepts like optics and mechanics.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

Designed with "Kindness by Design," The Sign eliminates death and punishment. Failure results in a friendly "Excavator Claw" scoop-and-reset, ensuring the game remains accessible and enjoyable for players of all ages, including children.

KEY CAPABILITIES

• Symbolic Gameplay: A strictly wordless environment using universal signage visual language.
• Forgiving Mechanics: Kinematic movement with coyote-time and non-lethal failure resets.
• Educational Puzzles: Physics-based challenges covering mechanics, optics, and chemistry.
